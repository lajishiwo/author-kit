\section{Conclusion}
PIGeR investigates externally grounded explicit geometry as an input to continuous spatial reasoning. It integrates measured relative pose and frozen BIP3D RGB-D features through an adaptation of SpaceMind's CGMF, preserving the existing visual-token interface and 3DThinker's eight recurrent latents. The verified 10K experiment shows heterogeneous task effects and no established Overall improvement over B0. Its numerical advantage over equal-parameter GENERIC fusion is unresolved by the current paired interval. Completing matched later-checkpoint evaluations and input interventions is necessary to determine whether camera-specific conditioning provides reliable benefits, and whether those benefits depend on correct geometric evidence.
