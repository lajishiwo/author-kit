\section{Conclusion}
PIGeR integrates measured camera poses and frozen BIP3D RGB-D features into the visual prefix of 3DThinker through an adaptation of CGMF from SpaceMind. It preserves the visual-token interface and eight recurrent continuous latents. In the single-seed 30K comparison, camera-guided fusion scores below both GENERIC and B0 on Full. GENERIC achieves a higher Overall score than B0, although this comparison changes both inputs and trainable parameters. Task-level results and development substitutions show that the use of these inputs is uneven across tasks. They do not establish a benefit mediated by the latent states. These findings support separate evaluation of geometric input availability and camera-specific conditioning. Matched ablations and multiple seeds are needed to assess whether the task tradeoffs persist.
